The Gym in Winter Springs, FL

5,000 square feet of dedicated training space. Boxing ring. MMA cage walls. Zebra Athletics mats. Built for combat sports.

Detroit Fight Company began as Head Coach and owner Sean Goolsby's academy in Jacareí, São Paulo, Brazil. In March 2026 he moved it to Winter Springs, Florida, into the former UFC Gym Winter Springs space at the Promenade at Tuskawilla.

Inside Detroit Fight Company

Facility

Dedicated Jiu-Jitsu mat area with six inch elevated Zebra Athletics floating mats (green border, black center) and full wall padding. Separate Muay Thai and boxing training floor with cage wall fencing and heavy bags. 18 foot boxing ring. Men's and women's changing rooms and bathrooms.

What to Bring

Comfortable workout clothes, a water bottle, and a towel. For Jiu-Jitsu, a rash guard or fitted athletic top. We have loaner gear available while you get started, so there is no need to buy anything up front.

Parking

Free parking in the Promenade at Tuskawilla shopping center lot directly in front of the gym.
