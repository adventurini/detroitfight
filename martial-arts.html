Martial Arts at Detroit Fight Company

Detroit Fight Company is a martial arts school on Tuskawilla Road in Winter Springs. Adults train Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Muay Thai, boxing and MMA. Kids ages 5 to 14 train Kids Jiu-Jitsu and Youth Boxing after school.

The head coaches are an IBJJF Black Belt, a professional MMA and bare knuckle fighter, and a former professional boxer. The All Access membership covers every program, so you can train one art or all of them.

The gym is about 15 minutes from Oviedo and Casselberry and about 20 from Winter Park, Lake Mary and Longwood. Meet the coaches, check the schedule, review membership pricing, and book a free trial class when you are ready.

Where to Start

Which Martial Art Should You Start With?

  • You want to control and submit a partner without striking: start with Jiu-Jitsu.
  • You want to strike with hands, feet, knees and elbows: start with Muay Thai.
  • You want sharp hands, footwork and conditioning: start with boxing.
  • You want striking, takedowns and submissions in one class: that is MMA.
  • Your child is 5 to 14: start with Kids Jiu-Jitsu or Youth Boxing.

Still not sure? Book the free trial class and choose Not Sure Yet. The membership covers every program, so you are never locked into one art.

Straight Answers

Karate, Judo, Wrestling and Kickboxing

Karate, judo and wrestling are taught inside the MMA class, together with kickboxing, Muay Thai and Jiu-Jitsu. They do not run as separate classes.

For kickboxing on its own, Muay Thai is the class. It is Thai kickboxing, and it runs Monday and Wednesday, 7:30 PM to 8:30 PM.
