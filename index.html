Our Story

Detroit Grit.
Florida Ground.

Detroit Fight Company was born from a simple idea: combat sports should be taught by people who actually compete. Not fitness influencers. Not franchise operators. Fighters.

Sean Goolsby, Head Coach and owner, is an IBJJF black belt who grew up in Detroit and built his career on the international stage. Detroit Fight Company is the gym he envisioned when he was coming up. World class instruction, a tight community, zero ego.

Detroit Fight Company began as Head Coach and owner Sean Goolsby's academy in Jacareí, São Paulo, Brazil. In March 2026 he moved it to Winter Springs, Florida, into the former UFC Gym Winter Springs space at the Promenade at Tuskawilla.

Whether you've never thrown a punch or you're training for your next competition, you belong here.
