Sean Goolsby

Head Coach and owner

Jiu-Jitsu

IBJJF black belt, European No Gi Champion and Pan Am Gi Champion.
Coaches adult Gi and No Gi Jiu-Jitsu and private lessons.

Read more

Sean didn't learn Jiu-Jitsu in Michigan. He learned it in Manaus, Brazil, training under brothers Cristiano and Angelo Carioca, competing within his first three months, and earning his blue and purple belts in one of the most serious training environments in the Amazon.

The path led to São Paulo and nearly seven years under Claudio Calasans, a multiple time World Champion and one of the most complete grapplers alive. That's where Sean earned his brown and black belts. That's where he became a professional.

From São Paulo to Hungary, training under Max Carvalho and winning the 2022 European IBJJF No Gi Championship at black belt Ultra Heavy in his international debut. Then to the UAE, competing for Baniyas Club, teaching under Palm Sports, and competing across the AJP Tour through Europe and North Africa. By season's end he was ranked #1 No Gi in the World and #4 Overall on the AJP Tour.

Back in Michigan in late 2023, he connected with David Garmo and Assembly Jiu-Jitsu, a partnership that sharpened his No Gi game to another level.

Detroit Fight Company began as Head Coach and owner Sean Goolsby's academy in Jacareí, São Paulo, Brazil, opened in 2023. In March 2026 he moved it to Winter Springs, Florida, into the former UFC Gym Winter Springs space at the Promenade at Tuskawilla.

Jiu-Jitsu gave Sean direction when he needed it, discipline when it was hard, and a community that spanned the world. Detroit Fight Company exists to do the same for others: not just to teach technique, but to change lives. That's been the mission from day one.

Pat Washington

Head Boxing Coach

Boxing

Former professional boxer, 20 and 1 with a WBF Intercontinental title.
Coaches boxing and conditioning.

Read more

Pat Washington is the heart and soul of Detroit Fight Company's boxing program. A former professional boxer with a record of 20 and 1 with 15 knockouts, Pat captured the WBF Intercontinental Light Welterweight Championship in just his ninth professional fight. He showed the technical skill and two handed power that had observers calling him one of the best kept secrets in American boxing.

Before turning pro, Pat served as a paratrooper in the U.S. Army's 82nd Airborne Division at Fort Bragg, where he represented the base as a competitive boxer. His professional career took him from the small show circuit through the Carolinas to a main event title fight, where he earned a clear unanimous decision for the belt.

But Pat's legacy extends far beyond his own career in the ring. He has dedicated decades to developing young boxers, not just as fighters but as people. At Detroit Fight Company, Pat brings a lifetime of experience, discipline, and genuine care for every athlete who steps through the door.

Professional record on BoxRec: 20 wins, 1 loss, 15 knockouts, 1994 to 1997.

Paulo Games

Head Muay Thai Coach

Muay Thai · Jiu-Jitsu

Professional MMA and bare knuckle fighter, black belt in Muay Thai, Jiu-Jitsu and Kickboxing.
Leads the Muay Thai program.

Read more

Paulo Games is a professional MMA and Bare Knuckle fighter whose career has been defined by an aggressive, technical fighting style and a willingness to take on the toughest opponents in combat sports. He holds black belts in Muay Thai, Jiu-Jitsu, and Kickboxing, with deep experience in Wrestling and Boxing. A complete combat athlete in every dimension.

Before relocating to Florida, Paulo owned and operated his own martial arts academy in Brazil, where he developed students from total beginners through high level competitors. That background as a gym owner gives him a perspective most pro fighters don't have: he knows how to build a fighter from the ground up, and he understands what it takes to make a beginner feel welcome on day one.

His recent record includes two consecutive BKFC wins in 2025, at BKFC 80 in Hollywood, Florida and BKFC 84 in Palm Desert, California, followed by a split decision win at Gamebred Bareknuckle MMA 10 in Miami on May 1, 2026, the promotion run by former UFC star Jorge Masvidal. He has 31 professional MMA bouts since 2012, listed on the stat sites under the name Paulo Machado. Across his career, Paulo has earned a reputation for durability, striking power, and a warrior mentality that shows up in his hardest fights.

At Detroit Fight Company, Paulo leads the Muay Thai program with the same standards he brings to his own training: discipline, technical precision, and a real fight IQ approach to every class. He works with students of all levels: beginners, kids, and athletes preparing to compete. Private lessons available by appointment.

Xavier Torres

Jiu-Jitsu Coach

Jiu-Jitsu

Second degree black belt with 18 years on the mat and 10 years coaching.
Coaches adult Jiu-Jitsu.

Read more

Xavier Torres is a second degree black belt, promoted by Boris Yee in 2018 under the EDJ affiliation, with 18 years on the mat and 10 years coaching. Before Winter Springs he ran EDJ Guayaquil as its head coach and owner in his hometown of Guayaquil, Ecuador, where he promoted 13 black belts, among them local league champions and nationally ranked athletes.

He competes in Gi and No Gi, and the Gi is home. At the 2016 IBJJF Spanish National Championship in Madrid he won gold in Gi, silver in No Gi and bronze in the No Gi absolute. In 2022 he won both qualifying stages of the Jiu-Jitsu League in Guayaquil and took the Grand Prix Super Champion title. He has fought in the cage too: a 2021 DFL main event, won by rear naked choke in the second round and named Fight of the Night.

Xavier started in MMA to learn to defend himself. He lost his first cage fight to a Jiu-Jitsu practitioner, took up the gentle art, and never put it down. What he wants every student to leave his class with is simple: confidence in themselves.

John Venturini

Jiu-Jitsu Coach

No Gi Jiu-Jitsu

Purple belt competitor with IBJJF No Gi medals at Nationals, Pan Ams and Worlds.
Coaches Jiu-Jitsu, with a focus on kids.

Read more

In 2021, John stepped onto the mats for the first time at 290 lbs with a simple conviction: if he was going to ask his kids to compete in combat sports, he had to be willing to do it himself.

What started as leading by example became a complete transformation. Over 100 lbs lost, a competition purple belt earned, and a growing collection of IBJJF hardware, including gold at No Gi American Nationals, silver at the Pan American No Gi Championships, and two bronze medals at the No Gi World Championships.

But the medals aren't the point. John's real passion is coaching kids. In a world of endless screens and manufactured comfort, he believes combat sports offer something irreplaceable: real challenge, real consequence, real growth. The kind of resilience that can't be downloaded. He started this journey to show his own children what was possible. Now he's Detroit Fight Company's biggest cheerleader, and the guy making sure every kid who walks through the door gets that same opportunity.

Luis

Kids Jiu-Jitsu Coach

Jiu-Jitsu

Marine veteran and wrestler, IBJJF Open winner at blue belt.
Coaches the kids Jiu-Jitsu program.

Read more

Wrestling took over Luis's life at 15 years old. After graduating high school with college wrestling scholarships in hand, he chose a different path: four years of service in the United States Marines, where he became a shooting instructor and rose to the rank of Sergeant.

After the military, Luis found Jiu-Jitsu and earned his blue belt while competing across multiple organizations, winning an IBJJF Open, two NAGAs, and a New Breed tournament, with additional podium finishes along the way. He's also spent the past year sharpening his boxing alongside the Detroit Fight Company coaching staff.

Luis brings a wrestler's work ethic and a Marine's discipline to coaching the next generation. He also serves as a high school wrestling coach, and at Detroit Fight Company he channels that experience into developing young athletes through the kids Jiu-Jitsu program.
